Giant Bedroom VR

An immersive VR experience that turns an ordinary bedroom into a giant-scale environment for navigation, discovery, and problem solving.

Overview

The project explores how familiar household objects can become navigation tools when the player is reduced to a tiny scale.

Challenge

The main challenge was creating a readable path through the room while keeping the interaction simple and visually clear.

Solution

Environmental clues guide the player through a sequence of simple interactions: a glowing phone, a frog ride, a scarf climb, tissue gliding, magnets, and a final medicine-bottle task.

Outcome

The final concept creates a clear progression while keeping the experience focused on scale, movement, and environmental storytelling.
